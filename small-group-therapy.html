Group programme

Small Group Therapy

A structured 12-week psychoeducation programme exploring themes of boundaries, consent, relationships and self-understanding. Open to everyone. No clinical referral or previous therapy experience needed.

This is a psychoeducation and personal development group. It is not a substitute for individual therapy or crisis support.

Next cohort starts Wednesday 6 January 2027 at 6pm UK time and runs on Wednesday evenings until 17 March. Bookings close Friday 18 December. Joining from outside the UK? See the start time in your time zone. Places are limited and the group runs once eight people have confirmed. Book your place below, or register your interest if you'd like the full details first. There's nothing to pay for that.

What the programme covers

  • Understanding yourself and your patterns
  • Boundaries: what they are, why they matter and how to set them
  • Consent: in relationships, with yourself and with others
  • Relationships: connection, communication and repair
  • Self-compassion and personal growth
