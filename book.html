Bookings by enquiry only · from £570

"The best investment you can make is in the time, love and effort you give to your relationship."

Intensive sessions are arranged directly. Get in touch to discuss your needs, availability and which package is right for you. I will respond within 2 working days.

  • 3 hours£570
  • 1 day£1,020
  • 2 days£1,980
  • 3 days + follow-up£2,950

Deposit & cancellation

  • Full payment is taken when your intensive is confirmed.
  • 25% of the fee is a non-refundable deposit (3 hours £142.50 · 1 day £255 · 2 days £495 · 3 days £737.50).
  • Cancel with more than 48 hours' notice: the remaining 75% is refunded.
  • Cancel with less than 48 hours' notice, or don't attend: the full fee is charged.
  • For multi-day packages the 48 hours runs from the start of the first day.
