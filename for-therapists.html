Option 01: One-to-one

Individual Supervision

One-to-one clinical supervision for qualified and trainee therapists. Supervision training completed at The Manchester Institute for Psychotherapy (2015), with further training in Group Supervision and a certificate in LGBTQ+ informed Supervision.

Supervision is not only for exploring client work but also for support for the practitioner's own wellbeing, including vicarious trauma, burnout, compassion fatigue, building your practice, and imposter syndrome.

Who is it for?

  • Qualified therapists in private practice
  • Trainees on courses and clinical placements
  • NHS practitioners
  • Specialists in drug & alcohol, eating disorders and other settings
  • LGBTQ+ informed supervision available
  • All sessions online via Zoom

Approach

Supervision draws primarily on Transactional Analysis, Relational and Integrative approaches, and the Developmental Model for Couples Therapy. The approach is adapted to the needs of each supervisee and their client work.

New to couples work? No problem. Supervisees at all stages of experience with couples are welcome.

Option 03: Community · coming early 2027

Healing Relationships

Healing Relationships is a new online community for therapists working with couples and relationships, regular, accessible learning and peer connection, without a long-term commitment. It is currently being built and will be launching in early 2027.

Join the mailing list below and you'll be the first to hear when it's nearing launch, with details of how to sign up.

What's planned

  • Regular live online sessions with Claire
  • A growing library of pre-recorded educational resources
  • Peer connection with other couples and relationship therapists
  • A simple rolling monthly format, no long-term lock-in

Details, pricing and start date will be confirmed closer to launch.
