Individual therapy

A space that is yours

I offer integrative, relational and trauma-informed therapy. Drawing on Transactional Analysis, Relational Psychotherapy and Nervous System Regulation, I work with each person to find an approach that fits them.

Sessions are 50 minutes, held online via Zoom. Payment is taken at the time of booking.

Wherever you are

Working with me from outside the UK

I work with people all over the world: British expats, English speakers living abroad, and international couples and therapists who want a UK-trained, BACP-registered psychotherapist. Everything happens online, so the process is the same wherever you are.

Sessions & time zones

Sessions are held on Zoom. When you book, the calendar shows availability in your own time zone, and your confirmation email includes the Zoom link. Times quoted on this site are UK time (GMT / BST).

Fees & payment

Fees are in pounds sterling (GBP) and paid securely by card at the time of booking. Your bank converts to your local currency at its own rate. There is nothing extra to arrange.

A few countries I can't work in

Because of professional and insurance requirements there is a small number of countries where I am unable to offer therapy or supervision. I check this when I receive your enquiry or booking, and if it applies to you I will let you know straight away and refund any payment in full.

Not sure? Just ask

If you would like to check before booking, send me a message with the country you live in and I will confirm within two working days.
