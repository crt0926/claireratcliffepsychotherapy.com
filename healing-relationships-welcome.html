1. Look out for your welcome pack

Within a couple of days of your form arriving I'll email you a short welcome pack: how the sessions run, the Zoom link we use every time, and the theme we're starting with. Before each session you'll get a reminder with the link, so there's nothing to write down.

2. The sessions

First and third Wednesday of each month, 6pm to 7.30pm UK time, on Zoom. The first one is Wednesday 6 January 2027. Nobody is asked to speak, and you can put questions in the chat. I'd encourage you to have your camera on: it helps us feel connected as a group. Use headphones if that would help you feel more private.

3. Worksheets and catch-up recordings

Each month's worksheet comes by email. If you miss a session, a view-only recording (only me on screen) follows within a day or two and works for two weeks.

4. Your membership

£65 a month, taken automatically. To update your card, see invoices or cancel at the end of your current month, use manage my membership (it asks for the email you paid with). The same link is on the Healing Relationships page whenever you need it.

If anything isn't clear, or the welcome pack hasn't arrived within three days of sending your form (do check your junk folder first), just email me at claireratcliffetherapy@gmail.com. I'm really looking forward to working with you.
